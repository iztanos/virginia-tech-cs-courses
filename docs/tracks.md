# Recommended elective sets by goal

[← back to index](../README.md)

The degree gives you roughly five discretionary slots: one Theory Elective, one CS 4/5XXX Elective, two
CS 3/4/5XXX Electives, one CS Technical Elective, plus the Capstone. These are opinionated ways to spend
them.

Every path below assumes the fixed core: CS 1114 → 2114 → 2505 → 2506 → 3114 → 3214, plus CS 2104, 3304,
3604, 1944, 3900, 4944.

---

## Backend / infrastructure SWE

The default good path, and the one that maps most directly to what most graduates actually do.

| Slot | Course | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Core anchor | [CS 3214](3000-level.md#cs-3214-computer-systems) | Non-negotiable. Concurrency, memory, sockets, performance |
| Elective | [CS 4604](4000-level.md#cs-4604-introduction-to-database-management-systems) | Best usefulness-to-pain ratio in the catalog |
| Elective | [CS 4254](4000-level.md#cs-4254-computer-network-architecture-and-programming) | Fills the biggest gap CS 3214 leaves |
| Theory | [CS 4104](4000-level.md#cs-4104-data-and-algorithm-analysis) | Check the instructor first — see [professors](professors.md) |
| Capstone | [CS 4284](4000-level.md#cs-4284-systems--networking-capstone) | Most technically substantial capstone |
| Optional | [CS 4224](4000-level.md#cs-4224-linux-kernel-programming) | The differentiator. Spring only, 0% withdrawal |
| Cheap add | [CS 2304](2000-level.md#cs-2304-topics-in-programming-systems) (SQL or JavaScript) | One credit, one concrete skill |

## Security

| Slot | Course | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Core anchor | [CS 3214](3000-level.md#cs-3214-computer-systems) | Prerequisite for everything below |
| Elective | [CS 4264](4000-level.md#cs-4264-principles-of-computer-security) | No bad instructor option — tightest spread in the department |
| Elective | [CS 3274](3000-level.md#cs-3274-software-reverse-engineering) | Highest withdrawal rate in the dataset (18.8%). Spring only. Worth it here |
| Elective | [CS 4224](4000-level.md#cs-4224-linux-kernel-programming) | Kernel-level understanding is rare and valuable in security |
| Capstone | [CS 4274](4000-level.md#cs-4274-secure-computing-capstone) | **Corequisite with CS 4264** — take them the same semester |

Skip [CS 2164](2000-level.md#cs-2164-foundations-of-contemporary-security-environments) and
[CS 4164](4000-level.md#cs-4164-future-of-security) unless the Secure Computing major requires them. Both are
policy courses with minimal technical content.

## ML / data

| Slot | Course | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elective | [CS 4824](4000-level.md#cs-4824-machine-learning) | The rigorous option. **Not** CS 3804 if this is the career |
| Elective | [CS 4654](4000-level.md#cs-4654-intermediate-data-analytics-and-machine-learning) | Methods in practice; complements 4824 rather than replacing it |
| Elective | [CS 4604](4000-level.md#cs-4604-introduction-to-database-management-systems) | Everyone in data works with databases |
| Theory | [CS 4104](4000-level.md#cs-4104-data-and-algorithm-analysis) | Complexity intuition matters more in ML than people expect |
| Capstone | [CS 4664](4000-level.md#cs-4664-data-centric-computing-capstone) or [CS 4094](4000-level.md#cs-4094-computer-science-capstone) | 4664 is usually restricted to DCC majors — check eligibility |

Take **MATH 3134** seriously rather than as a checkbox. It is the mathematical floor for CS 4824.

[CS 3654](3000-level.md#cs-3654-introductory-data-analytics-and-visualization) is the prerequisite for
CS 4654 — cheap and well-liked, but shallow on its own.

enroll.

## Maximum GPA, minimum learning

Documented because the data makes it obvious, not because it is a good idea.

| Course | GPA | Withdraw |
|---|---:|---:|
| [CS 3704](3000-level.md#cs-3704-intermediate-software-design-and-engineering) (Alsafwan) | 3.99 | 1% |
| [CS 3724](3000-level.md#cs-3724-introduction-to-human-computer-interaction) (McCrickard) | 3.91 | 0.0% |
| [CS 3604](3000-level.md#cs-3604-professionalism-in-computing) (Dunlap) | 3.79 | 0.5% |
| [CS 4634](4000-level.md#cs-4634-design-of-information) | 3.32 | 0.0% |
| [CS 4114](4000-level.md#cs-4114-introduction-to-formal-languages-and-automata-theory) | 3.27 | 0.3% |

Every one of these is above a 3.27 with sub-1% withdrawal. You will graduate with a strong GPA and
noticeably less to say in an interview.

## Sequencing notes that catch people out

- **CS 3114 cannot double-count.** It carries Pathways 5A but is the one course in the degree barred from double-counting. Plan an extra Pathways slot.
- **The capstone gate is CS 2506 + CS 3114**, effective Fall 2025. If either slips, your capstone slips a semester.
- **CS 4274 is a corequisite with CS 4264**, not a follow-on.
- **CS 4304 (Compilers) and CS 4204 (Graphics) do not run every year.** If you want them, watch the timetable from your sophomore year.
- **CS 4604 requires senior standing** — you cannot front-load the most useful elective in the catalog.
- **Spring only:** CS 3274, CS 4224, CS 3804, CS 1044. **Fall only:** CS 3314, CS 3824, CS 1054, CS 4944.
- **CS 3900 Bridge Experience** needs departmental approval of your plan before the experience, not after.
